WHAT IS PROBLEMATIC
SMARTPHONE USE?

Problematic Smartphone Use (PSU) refers to an inability to regulate one's use of mobile devices, leading to negative psychological and behavioral consequences in daily life.

In an era where our devices demand constant attention, understanding the boundary between healthy utility and clinical dependency is the first step toward regaining focus, improving sleep quality, and restoring your emotional baseline.

THE SYMPTOMS
Characterized by compulsive checking, losing track of time, and feeling anxious or fretful when separated from your device.
HOW IT AFFECTS YOU
Excessive screen time is clinically linked to poor sleep quality, elevated social anxiety, and depressive symptoms.
